# ECHO Web Assistant

**E.C.H.O.** (Executive Computer Heuristics Operator) is a highly intelligent, voice-first autonomous browser assistant designed to fundamentally change how you interact with the web. Instead of manually clicking, typing, and searching, ECHO takes control of your browser to perform complex tasks entirely on its own.

Built from the ground up by Deepak, ECHO leverages cutting-edge DOM manipulation, token-optimized machine vision, and real-time speech synthesis to act as a true digital companion inside your browser.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Zero-Cost Multi-Brain Architecture:** ECHO is built to run on **5 different AI models**, allowing you to use 100% free API endpoints so you never have to worry about subscription costs or tokens.
* **Autonomous Browser Control:** ECHO doesn't just tell you how to do things—it actually physically clicks buttons, types in search bars, and navigates web pages for you.
* **Token-Optimized DOM Vision:** Instead of expensive image processing, ECHO extracts a highly optimized Accessibility Tree of your screen in milliseconds, understanding exactly where every button and link is located.
* **Long-Term Memory:** ECHO has a built-in memory bank stored locally in your browser. It automatically remembers your preferences, facts, and templates across all tabs and sessions.
* **Floating Global Reactor:** Access ECHO via a sleek, draggable, glass-morphic "Reactor" orb that floats persistently on your screen, ready to listen at any time.

## 🧠 Supported AI Brains (100% Free Options Available)

ECHO supports seamless switching between the most powerful models in the world:

1. **Groq:** Ultra-fast, instant inference using Llama 3.3. (Highly Recommended - Free)
2. **OpenRouter:** Access to DeepSeek V3, Gemma 3, and more through dynamic auto-routing. (Free)
3. **Together AI:** Powerful open-source Llama and Mixtral endpoints. (Free)
4. **Google Gemini:** Supports Gemini 1.5 Pro, 2.0 Flash, and more.
5. **Anthropic Claude:** The gold standard for reasoning (Claude 3.5 Sonnet).

### How to Change the Brain & API Keys
1. **Right-click** on the ECHO Extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.
2. Click **Options**.
3. Select your preferred Brain Provider from the dropdown menu.
4. Paste your API Key (links to get free keys are provided right in the menu!).
5. Click **Save Settings**. ECHO will instantly switch to the new brain.

## 🕹️ How to Operate ECHO

ECHO is designed to be completely frictionless.

1. **Waking ECHO:** 
   - Left-click the ECHO Extension icon in your toolbar, OR
   - Press the keyboard shortcut: **Cmd + Shift + E** (Mac) or **Ctrl + Shift + E** (Windows).
2. **Interacting:**
   - Click the floating **Reactor Orb** to activate the microphone.
   - Speak your command naturally.
   - ECHO will process your request, speak its response back to you, and execute actions on the screen.
3. **Positioning:**
   - You can click and drag the Reactor orb to move it anywhere on your screen. It will remember its position even if you switch tabs.

## 🗣️ Example Commands

ECHO understands natural language. Here is what you can ask it to do:

* **Reading & Summarizing:**

* **Autonomous Navigation:**

* **Form Filling:**
  > *"Type 'Chicken Pasta Recipe' into the search bar."*

* **Visual Analysis:**
  > *"Take a screenshot and tell me what color the main banner is."*
